What the tab is for

The Overview tab gives the whole selection at a glance, counted in NCs: each NC counts once, whatever its number of defects. It answers four questions: how many NCs, are we on target, is anything out of the ordinary, and how fast are NCs closed?

Reading each chart

Key figures. NCs, defects, defects per NC, cost of non-quality, and NCs per MSN, for the selection. NCs per MSN has a switch, A330 + A350 or A320: the A320's products are small parts counted one by one and cannot share a rate with the other lines.

Each line against its target. One bar per line: its NCs per aircraft built this year. The black tick is the target, the small circle last year's rate, the percentage the gap to the target (red when above).

Cumulative NCs over the year. The NCs added month after month, against the target and last year. Dashed line: the target to date. Grey line: last year. Green dot: a month under its target; red: above. Dotted line: the forecast to December.

Cumulative cost of non-quality. The same curve in euros, with its own forecast and target. Read it like the curve of NCs.

How long an NC stays open. For each kind of decision, the share of NCs still open, day after day after creation. Each curve starts at 100 % and goes down as NCs close. The dot is the day half of them are closed (for example 7 days for a rework).

NCs per week, with their limits. The NCs of each week, around the average week. Dotted lines: the attention level. Red dashed lines: the alarm level. Shutdown weeks are left out of the calculation.

NCs by programme. The share of each line in the NCs. One bar per line.

Other lists and charts

  • NCs by product: Which products carry the NCs.
  • Decision and status: What was decided, and how many NCs are still open.
  • NCs opened and closed per month: Whether the stock of open NCs grows or shrinks.
  • Cost split by NC type: Which kind of NC (cable, test, final inspection, internal, supplier) carries the cost.
  • Liability: Who is liable for the NCs in SAP.
  • SAP defect code, SAP cause: The NCs by the codes typed in SAP.
  • Defect calendar: The defects day by day: the rhythm of the weeks, and the days with none (holidays, shutdowns).

NC detail

At the bottom, ▸ NC detail lists the NCs behind the tab, the most recent first: date, line, product, type, decision, status, station and title. Search it by NC number, word or FIN.
