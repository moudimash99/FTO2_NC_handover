What the tab is for

The Overview tab gives the whole selection at a glance, counted in NCs: each NC counts once, whatever its number of defects. It answers four questions: how many NCs, are we on target, is anything out of the ordinary, and how fast are NCs closed?

The charts

Key figures

NCs, defects, defects per NC, cost of non-quality, and NCs per MSN, for the selection.

  • Read itNCs per MSN has a switch, A330 + A350 or A320: the A320's products are small parts counted one by one and cannot share a rate with the other lines.
  • Use it tothe size of the selection in one line, before reading any chart.

Each line against its target

One bar per line: its NCs per aircraft built this year.

  • Read itThe black tick is the target, the small circle last year's rate, the percentage the gap to the target (red when above).
  • Use it tothe quickest way to see which line needs attention.
  • ClickKeeps that line on the whole page.

Cumulative NCs over the year

The NCs added month after month, against the target and last year.

  • Read itDashed line: the target to date. Grey line: last year. Green dot: a month under its target; red: above. Dotted line: the forecast to December.
  • Use it toshows whether the year is on track, and since which month it drifts.
  • ClickKeeps the NCs created that month.

Cumulative cost of non-quality

The same curve in euros, with its own forecast and target.

  • Read itRead it like the curve of NCs.
  • Use it toshows whether the cost follows the number of NCs, or grows faster because of a few expensive cases.
  • ClickKeeps that month.

How long an NC stays open

For each kind of decision, the share of NCs still open, day after day after creation.

  • Read itEach curve starts at 100 % and goes down as NCs close. The dot is the day half of them are closed (for example 7 days for a rework).
  • Use it toshows where closing takes time.
  • ClickKeeps the NCs still open after that number of days.

NCs per week, with their limits

The NCs of each week, around the average week.

  • Read itDotted lines: the attention level. Red dashed lines: the alarm level. Shutdown weeks are left out of the calculation.
  • Use it toseparates a week that is out of the ordinary from normal ups and downs.
  • ClickKeeps that week.

NCs by programme

The share of each line in the NCs.

  • Read itOne bar per line.
  • Use it tothe weight of each line at a glance.
  • ClickKeeps that line on the whole page.

Other lists and charts

Under ▸ Other lists and charts:

  • NCs by product: Which products carry the NCs.
  • Decision and status: What was decided, and how many NCs are still open.
  • NCs opened and closed per month: Whether the stock of open NCs grows or shrinks.
  • Cost split by NC type: Which kind of NC (cable, test, final inspection, internal, supplier) carries the cost.
  • Liability: Who is liable for the NCs in SAP.
  • SAP defect code, SAP cause: The NCs by the codes typed in SAP.
  • Defect calendar: The defects day by day: the rhythm of the weeks, and the days with none (holidays, shutdowns).

NC detail

At the bottom, ▸ NC detail lists the NCs behind the tab, the most recent first: date, line, product, type, decision, status, station and title. Search it by NC number, word or FIN.
