What the tab is for

The Cables tab shows the requests for new cables recorded by the cable team: a cable too short, damaged, missing, cut or not conforming. These requests do not come from SAP but from the cable team's tracker, saved with each update. The tab follows the Aircraft and Period filters only. The key figures give the number of requests, the share due to printing, the lines with a cause, the cables asked for again, and the lines with an action.

The charts

Requests per month and imputation

The requests of each month, split by who they are imputed to: the production line, the printing (machine or operator), the definition. The share due to printing is given per quarter. It shows whether the requests grow, and whether the balance between causes shifts.

Reason × imputation

A table of each reason (short, damaged, missing, cut, not conforming) against each imputation. It shows, for example, where the short cables come from: the line, the printing machine or the definition.

Cable types

The cable types asked for most, split by imputation. It points to the types worth a closer look.

Causes (proposed)

The cause proposed for each request, read in the cable team's analysis comment. Unknown – to analyse counts the requests with no cause yet: a high number there means the analysis is behind.

Recurring cables

The cables asked for again on several aircraft: a repeated request usually points to a lasting cause rather than a one-off mistake.

Requests

The list of the requests, with a search box. A click on a reason, an imputation, a cause or a cable in the cards above keeps only those in the list; a label at the top shows the choice.
