What the tab is for

The Cost tab answers: what do the NCs cost FTO2, and in what? The cost is an estimate built from the time each NC takes, at the hourly rate of who does the work: it is made to compare causes, lines and months, not to replace an accounting figure. The method is explained in How the cost is calculated.

The line under the title gives the whole picture: the total cost for FTO2, the part raised at FTO2, the customer returns, and beside them the quality aléas that led to no NC. The key figures give the cost, the number of NCs and the average cost per NC.

The charts

Cost split by NC type

One bar split by type of NC: cable NCs, test, final inspection, internal, suppliers. It shows which kind of NC carries the cost.

Decision, Line, Product, Month created

The cost from four angles: by decision taken (rework, rejection, scrap…), by line, by product and by month. A click on a bar filters the other cards of the tab and the list, to follow one part of the cost.

What the cost is made of, month by month

Each month split into the handling of the NC (alert, writing, support, recheck), the rework, the material and the engineering studies. It shows whether a rise comes from more NCs, from heavier reworks, or from parts and studies.

Quality aléas with no NC

The time lost declared in MES on quality aléas that did not lead to an NC: hours lost per month and per line, and their cost at the production rate. These are shown beside the cost of non-quality and are not added to it. When an aléa does lead to an NC, its time is already part of that NC's cost. The list of the aléas, one per row, is under the card.

Other cost charts

Under ▸ Other cost charts: every NC over time as a bubble at the size of its cost, with the five most expensive named, which shows whether the cost comes from many small NCs or a few big ones; and How the rework is costed, which says how many NCs are costed from the planned time of their rework order and how many from the model.

Explain the calculation

Under ▸ Explain the calculation, the whole calculation with the figures of your selection: the six steps of an NC with their time, rate and total; what they add up to; the special rules; what is counted for each decision; and the calculation on any real NC (type its number, or click an example). It is the place to answer "how was this number made?".

NC and cost detail

The list of the NCs with their cost, where they were found, their decision and their number of defects. Search it by NC number or word.
