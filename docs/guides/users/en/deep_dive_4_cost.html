What the tab is for

The Cost tab answers: what do the NCs cost FTO2, and in what? The cost is an estimate built from the time each NC takes, at the hourly rate of who does the work: it is made to compare causes, lines and months, not to replace an accounting figure. The method is in How the cost is calculated.

The line under the title gives the whole picture: the total cost for FTO2, the part raised at FTO2, the customer returns, and beside them the quality aléas that led to no NC. The key figures give the cost, the number of NCs and the average cost per NC.
