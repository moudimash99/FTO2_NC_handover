About the data

  • The data is as fresh as the last update. The date Data as of… at the top right is the day of the most recent NC. If it is old, the update has not been run; the figures are right, but stop at that date.
  • Deleted NCs are counted. The work they caused was done. This is one reason a total can differ from another report; ⓘ How the selection is made shows what each filter keeps and removes.
  • The A400M has no NC text in Skywise: its NCs count as NCs and in the cost, but no defect can be read from them.
  • The texts of the NCs stay in French, as written in SAP, also on the English page.

About the rates and targets

  • The A320 builds small parts counted one by one: its rate per unit is very low and its percentages jump with a few NCs. Read it in numbers of NCs.
  • The month in progress is judged on the days gone by and will still move.
  • A few NCs have a product that is not in the production plan. They count in every total, but not in the rates, the forecasts per product or the targets. The page lists them under the charts concerned.

About the causes

  • Each defect line is classified into a cause. An official sub-code written in the NC decides. Lines written or changed recently may be classified by simpler rules until the next classification: their cause can be less precise for a while.
  • If a defect is clearly in the wrong cause, give its NC number to the person in charge of the dashboard.

About the cost

  • The cost is an estimate made to compare, not an accounting figure (How the cost is calculated).
  • The value of a scrapped or exchanged part is counted only when its price is known.
  • The cost of customer returns is estimated by the same model; real invoices are not available.

About the bell

  • From January to late April the bell is quiet: it compares the last weeks with the weeks before, inside the year, and needs sixteen weeks of the year.
  • An alarm is a question to ask, not a verdict.

When the page does not open

  • It stays on the loading box, or a box names a query: wait a minute and reload. If it stays, tell the person in charge of the dashboard, with the text of the box.
  • It opens for others but stays empty for you: your account probably lacks access to the data. Ask the owner of the dashboard in Skywise.
