What the tab is for

The Overview tab gives the whole selection at a glance, counted in NCs: each NC counts once, whatever its number of defects. It answers four questions: how many NCs, are we on target, is anything out of the ordinary, and how fast are NCs closed?

The charts

Key figures

ShowsNCs, defects, defects per NC, cost of non-quality, and NCs per MSN, for the selection.
How to read itNCs per MSN has a switch, A330 + A350 or A320: the A320's products are small parts counted one by one and cannot share a rate with the other lines.
How it helpsThe size of the selection in one line, before reading any chart.

Each line against its target

ShowsOne bar per line: its NCs per aircraft built this year.
How to read itThe black tick is the target, the small circle last year's rate, the percentage the gap to the target (red when above).
How it helpsThe quickest way to see which line needs attention.
A clickKeeps that line on the whole page.

Cumulative NCs over the year

ShowsThe NCs added month after month, against the target and last year.
How to read itDashed line: the target to date. Grey line: last year. Green dot: a month under its target; red: above. Dotted line: the forecast to December.
How it helpsShows whether the year is on track, and since which month it drifts.
A clickKeeps the NCs created that month.

Cumulative cost of non-quality

ShowsThe same curve in euros, with its own forecast and target.
How to read itRead it like the curve of NCs.
How it helpsShows whether the cost follows the number of NCs, or grows faster because of a few expensive cases.
A clickKeeps that month.

How long an NC stays open

ShowsFor each kind of decision, the share of NCs still open, day after day after creation.
How to read itEach curve starts at 100 % and goes down as NCs close. The dot is the day half of them are closed (for example 7 days for a rework).
How it helpsShows where closing takes time.
A clickKeeps the NCs still open after that number of days.

NCs per week, with their limits

ShowsThe NCs of each week, around the average week.
How to read itDotted lines: the attention level. Red dashed lines: the alarm level. Shutdown weeks are left out of the calculation.
How it helpsSeparates a week that is out of the ordinary from normal ups and downs.
A clickKeeps that week.

NCs by programme

ShowsThe share of each line in the NCs.
How to read itOne bar per line.
How it helpsThe weight of each line at a glance.
A clickKeeps that line on the whole page.
