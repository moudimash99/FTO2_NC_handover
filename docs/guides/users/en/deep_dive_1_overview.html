What the tab is for

The Overview tab gives the whole selection at a glance, counted in NCs: each NC counts once, whatever its number of defects. It answers: how many NCs, are we on target, is anything out of the ordinary, and how fast are NCs closed?

The key figures

A row of numbers at the top: NCs, defects, defects per NC, cost of non-quality, and NCs per MSN. The last one has a small switch, A330 + A350 or A320, because the A320's products are small parts counted one by one and cannot be mixed with the other lines in one rate.

The charts

Each line against its target

One bar per line, the NCs per aircraft built this year, with a black tick for the target and a small circle for last year's rate. A bar past its tick is a line above its target. It is the quickest way to see which line needs attention.

Cumulative NCs over the year

The NCs added month after month, against the dashed line of the target and the grey line of last year. Green dots are months under their target, red dots months above; the dotted line is the forecast to December. It shows whether the year as a whole is on track, and since when it drifts.

Cumulative cost of non-quality

The same curve in euros, with its own forecast and target. It shows whether the cost follows the number of NCs, or grows faster because of a few expensive cases.

How long an NC stays open

For each kind of decision, the share of NCs still open day after day after their creation. The dot on each curve is the day half of them are closed (for example 7 days for a rework, 20 for a scrap or rejection). It helps to see where the closing takes time.

NCs per week, with their limits

The NCs of each week, with the average week and two levels around it: the attention level and the alarm level. A week beyond the alarm level is not an ordinary week and is worth a question. Shutdown weeks are left out of the calculation.

NCs by programme

The share of each line in the NCs. A click on a line keeps that line on the whole page.

Other lists and charts

Under ▸ Other lists and charts: NCs by product; by decision and status; NCs opened and closed per month (to see whether the open stock grows); the cost split by type of NC (cable NCs, test, final inspection, internal, suppliers); by liability; by SAP defect code and SAP cause; and a calendar of the defects, day by day, which shows the rhythm of the weeks and the days with no defect (public holidays, shutdowns).

NC detail

The list of the NCs behind the tab, the most recent first, with their date, line, product, type, decision, status, station and title. Search it by NC number, word or FIN.
