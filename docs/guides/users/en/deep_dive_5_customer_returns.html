What the tab is for

The Customer returns tab shows the NCs raised outside FTO2, by the assembly lines or the customers, for which FTO2 is liable. They are the defects that left the plant, so they matter more than their number suggests. The tab counts them in defects. The key figures give the number of returns, the share of damaged items, the share of physical damage, and their cost.

The charts

Category and Root cause (RCA)

The returns by category and by cause, as in the Pareto tab. They show which kinds of defect escape the plant, which can differ from those found inside it.

Type of damage

For the defects classified as a damaged item: what kind of damage. It helps to tell a handling or packing problem from a manufacturing one.

Line and Month created

The returns by line and by month. They show which line sends the most returns, and whether returns are rising.

Customer return detail

The list of the returns. A click on a row opens the full text of the NC, with the site that raised it: the most direct way to understand a return.

Good to know

  • A click on a bar filters the other cards of the tab and the list.
  • The filter FTO2 plant only has no effect here: this tab only shows NCs raised outside FTO2.
  • The cost of a return is estimated by the same model as the other NCs; real invoices are not available.
