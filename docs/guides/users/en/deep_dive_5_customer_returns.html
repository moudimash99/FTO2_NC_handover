What the tab is for

The Customer returns tab shows the NCs raised outside FTO2, by the assembly lines or the customers, for which FTO2 is liable. They are the defects that left the plant, so they matter more than their number suggests. The tab counts them in defects. The key figures give the number of returns, the share of damaged items, the share of physical damage, and their cost.

Good to know

  • The filter FTO2 plant only has no effect here: this tab only shows NCs raised outside FTO2.
  • The cost of a return is estimated by the same model as the other NCs; real invoices are not available.
