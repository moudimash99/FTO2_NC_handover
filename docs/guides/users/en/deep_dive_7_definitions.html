What the tab is for

The Definitions tab is the dictionary of the dashboard. Every chart that shows a category or a cause uses the words defined here. It is the place to settle "what exactly does this cause cover?", with figures and real examples.

How a defect gets its cause

A diagram at the top shows the chain: 123 official sub-codes are grouped into 42 sub-categories; each sub-category gives exactly one cause (25 in all); each cause belongs to exactly one category of the Pareto (9 in all). An official code written in the NC decides; when there is none, the text of the NC decides. When the text describes a cause and its consequence, the defect is classified on the cause.

Under the diagram, a table lists the causes with their figures. A click on a row goes to the definition of that cause.

The definition of each cause

The causes are grouped by category. For each cause the tab gives:

  • its definition in one or two sentences, in French and in English;
  • its number of defects, its share of all defects, its number of NCs, its cost, and its customer returns;
  • its sub-categories, with the official codes each one groups;
  • three real examples, with their NC numbers.

The examples are often the fastest way to see where the line between two neighbouring causes lies, for example between a swapped wire and a wrong terminal.

Good to know

  • The definitions are kept with the settings of the dashboard; if a definition needs to change, tell the person in charge of the dashboard.
  • If a defect seems to be in the wrong cause, give its NC number to the person in charge of the dashboard.
