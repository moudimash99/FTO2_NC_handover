The idea

The cost of non-quality is an estimate: the time each NC takes, at the hourly rate of who does the work, plus engineering studies and parts. It is not an invoice and not an accounting figure. It is made to compare: between causes, lines and months. The cost counts only where FTO2 is liable; the other NCs count as NCs, with a cost of 0 for FTO2.

The minutes and the hourly rates below are those used today. They are settings agreed with the team: when one changes, the costs of the whole year follow at the next update. The dashboard always shows the values in use, in Cost of non-quality › Explain the calculation.

Today's rates are 123 €/h for production, 107 €/h for quality and 76 €/h for methods. Setup and hands-on work together count at least 60 minutes. To these steps are added the engineering study when a design query is raised (8 hours at 78.16 €/h today) and the material of the defects.

What each decision counts

Not every NC goes through all six steps. A rework counts all of them; a deleted NC only the alert and the writing; a rejection or a scrap has no rework but adds the value of the part. The full table is in Explain the calculation › See what is charged for each decision.

The special rules

  • No alert for an NC found at test or final inspection; a customer return always has the alert.
  • Connection errors: the first in an NC counts 35 minutes, each following one 15 minutes.
  • Internal NCs: setup and hands-on work count twice.
  • When the NC's rework order is closed and has planned times, those times replace setup and hands-on work.
  • When the operator declared a quality aléa for the NC (same work order, from 2 hours before to 72 hours after), the time lost declared replaces the alert.
  • The value of a scrapped or exchanged part is added when its price is known.

Three levels, for comparison

For any NC, Explain the calculation shows three figures. Level 3 is the detailed cost above, the one the dashboard uses. Level 2 is the same NC at average times. Level 1 is the Airbus standard cost for its decision, whatever the number of defects. The gap between them shows how much the detail of each NC matters.

Seeing it on a real NC

In Cost of non-quality › ▸ Explain the calculation, type an NC number in NC number and click Show, or click one of the examples. The table gives each step, how its time was found, the minutes and the cost; under it, the defect lines of the NC with the minutes and the material each one adds.
