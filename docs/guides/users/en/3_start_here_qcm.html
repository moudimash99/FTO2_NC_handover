What you look for

You follow all the lines together, and you compare them. You want the general trend month after month, the lines that drift from their target, and the same picture for the series, REC and FTI flows. The dashboard's Essential mode was made for this monthly review.

If you have not used the dashboard yet, read the Introduction to the dashboard first (ten minutes).

The charts that help you most

Essential › Summary was made for the monthly review of all lines. The four key figures give the year at a glance: NCs since January, NCs per MSN, cost of non-quality, customer returns, each with its December forecast and its target.

Cumulative NCs over the year shows the curve of the year against the dashed line of the target: above it, more NCs than the target allows at this date. Green dots are months under their target, red dots months above. Each line against its target shows in one look which line drifts: a bar past its black tick is above its target.

Monthly ranking of root causes and Root causes: 2025 against 2026 show which causes take more room, and which grew since last year. Volume or unit cost separates the causes that are a matter of volume (many defects, each cheap) from those that are a matter of unit cost (few defects, each expensive), which call for different actions.

In the Deep dive mode, Overview shows whether a week was out of the ordinary and how fast NCs are closed; Production shows the volumes behind the rates, when a rate surprises; Cost of non-quality shows what the cost is made of.

Series, REC and FTI

The page opens on series NCs. To look at the other flows, change the filter Production type: All, REC or FTI. With REC, a switch Cost appears: the cost of the NCs FTO2 is liable for, or of all of them. The targets are built on series production; read REC and FTI mainly in counts and trends.

Good to know

  • Compare rates, not counts. Lines and months differ in volume; NCs per MSN makes them comparable.
  • The A320 builds small parts counted one by one: its rate per unit is very low and its percentages jump with a few NCs. Read it in numbers of NCs. This is why targets are set line by line.
  • The A400M has no NC text in Skywise: its NCs count, but no defect can be read from them.
  • To show a chart in a meeting, click ⤢ Enlarge on it.
