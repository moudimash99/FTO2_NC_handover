What the dashboard shows

The dashboard shows the non-conformities (NCs) of FTO2: how many there are, on which lines, products and aircraft, from which causes, at what cost, and whether each line is on its target. It only shows: nothing you click changes an NC or any data.

The NCs come from SAP, through Skywise. The number of aircraft built comes from the production plan. The data is updated by the person in charge of the dashboard; the date Data as of… at the top right of the page says up to which day it goes.

Opening it

  1. Open the link of the dashboard, signed in to Skywise as usual. Keep it in your browser's favourites.
  2. Wait a few seconds: a green box says that the data is loading, then the page appears.
  3. The page opens in French. The EN button at the top right switches it to English; this guide uses the English words. The texts of the NCs themselves always stay in French.

If the page stays on the loading box, or shows a red box, see Known limits, or tell the person in charge of the dashboard.

The page from top to bottom

  • At the top right: Data as of…, the day of the most recent NC; the bell, what deserves a look this week (below); FR / EN, the language; and a large switch Essential / Deep dive, the two modes.
  • Under the title: the filters. Two or three rows of lists and buttons that decide which NCs you look at (below). Under them, a short line counts the NCs and defects kept, for example 868 NCs · 3,160 defects in the selection.
  • The tabs of the mode you are in, then the content of the tab: a row of key figures, then the charts, each in a white card with its title and one line that says how to read it.

Choosing what you look at

The filters decide which NCs are on screen; every figure of every tab follows them. As the page opens, it shows the series NCs raised at FTO2, for which FTO2 is liable, created since 1 January.

FilterWhat it does
AircraftOne line: A350, A330, A320, A400M; or all of them.
Production typeSeries, REC or FTI, or all of them.
PeriodYear to date, the last 30 days, the last full month (in November: all of October), the last 3, 6 or 12 months, all dates, or two dates you choose.
LiabilityWho is liable for the NC in SAP: FTO2, the suppliers, others.
FTO2 plant onlyYes: only the NCs raised at FTO2. No: also those raised elsewhere, such as customer returns.
Minor defectsCount or leave out the minor defects (a missing tie, cap or plug). Changes the number of defects, never the number of NCs.
Data sourceRarely needed: all NCs, Skywise only, or the NCs also on the GPS register or the Test list.

A click on a chart also filters. Click a line, a product, a month or an aircraft: a label appears at the end of the filters, for example Product: MIP A350 ✕, and the whole page follows; its ✕ removes it. In the Pareto, Cost and Customer returns tabs, a click on a bar filters the other cards of that tab only; Clear all removes those choices. Reset, at the right of the filters, puts everything back as the page opens.

Before you trust a total, read the line that counts the selection. If a number surprises you, click ⓘ How the selection is made: the page replays the filters one by one and shows what each one removed.

Downloading the NCs: at the bottom of every tab, ⤓ Short extract and ⤓ Full extract save the NCs of the selection in a file that Excel opens, one row per defect. The short one has the key columns (NC, date, line, MSN, product, station, category, cause, sub-code, FIN, text, cost); the full one adds the rest, the most useful first.

The bell

The bell does the weekly watch. Line by line, it compares the last four finished weeks with the twelve weeks before, taking into account the number of aircraft built, and lists what is rising more than chance can explain: a category, a cause, a sub-code, a station, a product, an aircraft with far more NCs than usual. A number on the bell gives how many alarms there are; it is red when one of them is a strong rise.

  • Click the bell to see the list, then click an alarm: the page opens the right tab, filtered on that problem.
  • An alarm is a question to ask, not a verdict: look at the NCs behind it.
  • It follows the Aircraft and Production type filters, not the period: it always looks at the latest weeks.
  • From January to late April it says Not enough weeks yet to compare: it needs sixteen weeks of the year.

Words you will meet

NCOne non-conformity in SAP. It counts once, whatever it holds. Deleted NCs are counted too.
DefectOne defect line inside an NC: an NC with 16 defect lines is 1 NC and 16 defects.
NCs per MSNThe rate: NCs divided by the aircraft and products built in the same period. It makes months and lines comparable.
TargetEach line's rate of last year, lowered by the cut decided for the year (25 % today).
Cost of non-qualityAn estimate of what the NCs cost: the time each one takes, at the hourly rate of who does the work. Made to compare, not an accounting figure.
Category, causeEach defect is classified into one cause (25 of them), which belongs to one of 9 categories. The Definitions tab explains each one.
