What the page is for

The MFT page serves the weekly review of one line: the Pareto of the week's defects, what rises and falls compared with the weeks before, and the follow-up of the MFT actions. It counts the NCs raised at FTO2 and their defects, over two years: the year followed and the one before. So a week, a month or an action is always compared with the weeks just before it, even across the new year. Data as of… is the day of the most recent NC. The page opens in French; EN, at the top right, switches to the English words this guide uses.

The controls at the top

LineOne line (A330, A350, A400M, A320), or All. At first, the line with the most NCs.
ProductionSeries (at first), REC, FTI, or All.
LiabilityA drop-down list: FTO2, Suppliers, Others, each with its number of NCs in the weeks shown. FTO2 is ticked at first, as on the dashboard; tick others to add them.
PeriodWeek, 1 month (4 weeks), 3 months (13 weeks) or Custom (from … to …), then the week, chosen in the list. At first, the last finished week.

Closure weeks come from the plant's blackout days: marked (shutdown) in the list and drawn pale, they stay out of every average and every reference.

Weekly Pareto

Four figures open the tab: NCs, Defects, Defects per NC, Top category. NCs and defects are compared, per week, with the weeks before: +18% vs 15.3 (W39–W42) means 18 % more than the average of weeks 39 to 42. A week is compared with the 4 working weeks before it, 1 month with the 3 months before, 3 months with the 12 months before. Count switches the bars between Defects and NCs.

Categories, Causes, Official sub-codes, FIN (reference)

ShowsThe defects of the period by family, then by cause, official sub-code and FIN, the largest first. The black tick on each bar: the average of the weeks before.
How to read itA bar past its tick: more than usual. In Categories the % is the running share, elsewhere each row's share. FINs that are product names (740VU…) are left out.
How it helpsFrom the family to the precise problem: the cause, its code, then where it happens.
A clickKeeps that value on the whole page (below).

Defects

How to read itOne row per defect: date, NC, MSN, station, FIN, cause. Show more adds rows.
How it helpsWhere a figure becomes real cases, to discuss in the review.

A click on a bar filters the whole page, the Analysis tab included: a cause, then a FIN, narrows it step by step. A label above the figures shows each choice; its ✕ removes it. Changing the line clears them.

Analysis

Defects per week, by category

ShowsThe 26 weeks up to the week chosen, one bar per week, coloured by category.
How to read itPale: a closure week or the week in progress. ▼ Action starts marks when each action of the line started.
How it helpsTells a peak from a trend, and shows what followed an action.

Rising, Falling

ShowsThe causes clearly up, or down: the last 4 working weeks against the 8 before.
How to read it2.1 → 4.3 / wk: 2.1 defects a week before, 4.3 now. Only clear changes are listed: at least a quarter up or down, on enough defects.
How it helpsShows a problem starting, or an action working, before the month ends.
A click+ Action opens a new action on that cause.

New this week, Recurring FINs

ShowsThe causes and sub-codes found this week and in none of the 8 working weeks before; the FINs found on 3 aircraft or more over the last 8 working weeks.
How to read itEach with its number of defects; a FIN with its main cause. Product names written as a FIN (740VU…) are left out.
How it helpsA new kind of defect, the first time it shows; the same place going wrong on several aircraft, often one cause to fix once.
A click+ Action, as above.

The bell

The bell, at the top right, does the weekly watch with the dashboard's rule: it compares the last four finished weeks with the twelve before, taking into account the aircraft built, and lists what rises more than chance can explain, for the line, week, production and liability chosen. Click an alarm to open the Weekly Pareto filtered on it; + Action opens a new action on it, Seen sets it aside. From January to late April it says Not enough weeks yet to compare: it needs sixteen weeks of the year.

Action tracking

The tab lists the MFT actions of the line: Open, All or Closed, summed up in one line such as 4 actions · 1 on target · 2 rising. Each card gives the action's status (To start, In progress, Closed, Dropped), its verdict, the Problem followed (the defects it targets), the owners, the dates and a chart.

How an action is judged

Against the weeks before it. The reference is the problem's average number of defects per week over the 8 working weeks before the action start (or the analysis start, until the action has a start date). The target is the reference minus 30 %. The current value is the average of the last 4 working weeks.

Target metAt or under the target for 4 weeks in a row: counted as on target.
Below targetUnder the target, for less than 4 weeks so far.
Falling, Rising, SteadyAt least 10 % under the reference, at least 10 % over it (counted as rising), or in between.
Too early to tell, No referenceFewer than 4 working weeks since the start, or before it.

Adding or changing an action

  1. Press + New action, or + Action beside a cause, a sub-code, a FIN or an alarm: the form then opens with that problem filled in.
  2. Fill in the form. For the Problem followed, Simple takes a code (Q11: all its sub-codes) or a sub-code (Q11-003); Expert takes conditions, such as a cause or a FIN. The form shows what the problem catches: check it.
  3. Press Create the action (Save for a change). Then, as after any change on a card (Edit, Close, Delete, Add the point for this week's point), press Submit in the save form below the page. Until you do, a grey note says the change is kept on this screen only; several changes can wait for one Submit. The actions are kept for everyone: everyone sees the change once the data is refreshed (reload the page).
