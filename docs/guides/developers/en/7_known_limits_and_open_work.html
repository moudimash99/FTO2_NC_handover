Known limits

These are known, and accepted for now. The users' document Known limits explains the ones they can see.

LimitWhat it means
New defect linesA defect line gets its precise cause when it is labelled. Until then it keeps the defect reader's own cause, which is a little less precise. Each update lists the lines still to label.
The A400MIts NCs reach Skywise without their text. One defect line is made from the title when a rule recognises it; otherwise the NC counts, with no defect.
The cost is an estimateMade to compare causes, lines and months, not to be booked. It has not been compared with the official Airbus method.
Part pricesMany parts have no price yet: their NCs are counted without the part's value. Each update lists them, most frequent first.
Material numbers in FusionA material number typed as a number loses its leading zeros. The update matches numbers without leading zeros, but keep the column as text in the sheet.
Early JanuaryThe year followed changes by itself on 1 January, so the dashboard starts almost empty. The bell stays quiet until the year has 16 finished weeks (late April).
Two copies of the bellThe dashboard and the MFT page each hold their own copy of the alarm engine. A change to one must be made to the other.
The 2026 targets2025 is counted in products delivered (the plan does not reach back to 2025), 2026 in products built over the working days: the rates differ by a few percent per line (about 6 % on the A320). From 2027 both years are measured from the plan, the same way.
Last year's NC typeBoth years go through the same steps, but only this year's GPS register is read: last year's NCs take their type from the rule table, this year's from the register when it lists them. The type changes a few cost rules (final inspection, internal work).
Deleted NCsCounted, by decision: the work they caused was done. They have their own NC type (Qualité), so they show as their own bar by place of detection. In the default view they are about 5 % of this year's NCs (8 % last year) and 1.5 % of the defects: too few to change the Pareto. Some are copies of a live NC, counted twice.
Last December's aléasRead only to link the NCs of early January; they are not counted as aléas with no NC.
