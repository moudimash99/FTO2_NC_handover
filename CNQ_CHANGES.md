## Open checks

One line per check in `05_outputs/CNQ_investigation_checklist.html`:

- **H1 (High)** Rework done without an NC (shop floor): Which are non-quality? Which should have had an NC?
- **H2 (High)** Quality team time booked on rework orders: Is there one per line every month? Which hours are NC handling (CNQ) and which are normal quality work? Overlap with the model's 'writing the NC' or 'technical support' steps?
- **H3 (High)** Customer returns: the actual cost, work at the customer included: Repair hours actually spent at the customer, analysis hours, transport / kits, penalties. Type them in; 'Cost with findings' replaces the model's rework by the real repair hours.
- **H4 (High)** BF24 / BF20 cable cutting for stock: A consequence of non-quality (cables remade because of defects), or a definition / kitting problem?
- **H5 (High)** Reworked NCs with no rework order: Fixed on the spot, or an order that exists but was never linked to the NC?
- **H6 (High)** Rework orders with no planned time: Why no time (created without times, closed without confirmation, time booked elsewhere)? How should those NCs be costed?
- **H7 (High)** Is the disruption export complete?: Re-export January-March and compare 5 January; look for a row limit.
- **M1 (Medium)** Other aléas that look like quality: Yes or no per group. Groups marked yes are added to the disruption block.
- **M4 (Medium)** ERMA aléas in the disruption block: Does each have an ERMA NC? If not, set Keep = Y so it counts.
- **M2 (Medium)** Rework orders naming NCs we do not have: Plant, liability and writer of those NCs: out of scope (ERMA, other plants), or missing from our export?
- **M3 (Medium)** Exceptional NCs (not for the presentation): Is the planned time right? Should such NCs be costed from their order?
- **D (Info)** Disruption block (option B): the list behind the figure: Nothing to do unless an aléa should not count: set Keep = N.
- **L2 (Low)** Internal NCs with no aléa logged in MES (later, not tomorrow): Why are most aléas of internal NCs not logged in MES? Confirm the assumption.
- **L1 (Low)** MSN 700/701 work (IEVB work centres): Confirm it is flight-test work and out of scope.
